আনন্দধারা · design conceptsFour directions for the Rabindrasangeet site, each rooted in Tagore and Santiniketan (D is C softened after feedback). Pick one, or mix.

Each mockup is a click-through prototype over the real docs/songs.json and docs/feelings.json: Listen (feelings box, season, moods, well-known songs), song page (Bengali lyrics, facts, recordings, Ask AI, More like this), the jukebox (player, controls, filters, playlist with singers), Browse with type-to-find, and Mine. Every concept also places the upcoming features: বাংলা/English switch, a persistent player bar with a progress line, the “Welcome back — continue?” prompt, the account menu with Google sign-in and “Watch without ads (YouTube Premium)”, and “Open in YouTube” on every recording. Resize to a phone width: each has a phone and a desktop layout, and light and dark modes (☾).

Concept Aপাণ্ডুলিপি

A · Pandulipi the Gitabitan manuscript

Aged paper, blue-black fountain-pen ink and a red margin rule. Tiro Bangla for titles and lyrics, Galada (brush script) only for the brand and the feelings heading, Alegreya for English. Every page is dated in the Bengali calendar the way Tagore dated his poems; moods are an index with dotted leaders (সূচীপত্র); browse tabs are notebook index tabs; the song page is a ruled leaf with the facts and recordings in the margin; Tagore’s strike-through doodles are the only ornament. Top text navigation on every width; a bookmark-ribbon player bar with a red hairline progress. Dark mode is “night ink”.

Concept Bঋতুরঙ্গ

B · Riturango the six seasons colour the site

Light, soft cards, Noto Serif Bengali display with Hind Siliguri. The current ritu sets the accent, the page tint and a drawn sky scene in the home hero (rain for Barsha, kash grass for Sharat, mustard fields for Sheet, palash for Basanta…); a season row lets you preview any ritu; a song page takes on its own song’s season. Moods are colour fields; well-known songs and “more like this” are thumbnail cards in horizontal rows; filters live in a bottom sheet. Bottom tabs and a floating player pill on the phone, a top bar and a full bar on desktop. Dark mode is the same seasons at night.

Concept Cরাঙামাটি

C · Rangamati Santiniketan’s red earth and alpona

Laterite terracotta, rice-paste white, khoai ochre, sal green and kantha indigo; Noto Serif Bengali 800 for display. Every mood, season, song and the brand gets a generated alpona medallion (rotational symmetry seeded from its name), a running alpona vine borders the terracotta band, kantha running-stitch rules divide sections, and songs are numbered like a songbook. The home panel knows Santiniketan’s days (Poush Mela, ২৫শে বৈশাখ, ২২শে শ্রাবণ, নববর্ষ). A terracotta sidebar on desktop, a terracotta band plus bottom tabs on the phone, a terracotta player bar. Dark mode is night under the chhatim.

Concept Dশান্ত রাঙামাটি

D · Rangamati, softened rice paper, soft ink, a dusty clay line

C after the feedback that it was too red and too ornamented. Rice-paper cream and warm grey-black ink; the laterite becomes a dusty clay accent used only for hairlines, small labels, the active state and one primary button. One single-weight line-drawn alpona as the mark and a hairline alpona divider in two places; moods are quiet text labels with a tiny leaf glyph; no medallions, vine borders or stitches. A’s ruled manuscript leaf carries the lyrics on the song page and in the jukebox; the current ritu is a pale watercolour wash behind the Bengali date in the home banner, no illustrated scene. Tiro Bangla titles at regular weight, generous space. Top bar on desktop, bottom tabs on the phone. Dark mode is soft night ink.

How to view. Serve the repository root (for example python3 -m http.server 8010 in the repo folder, or node design/serve.js if you prefer) and open http://localhost:8010/design/mockups/. The mockups fetch ../../docs/songs.json, so they must be served from the repo root, not from docs/.
  • All three share common.js (data, bilingual strings, Bengali date, search, playlist and player logic); only the look differs, so a chosen concept can borrow another’s layout ideas.
  • Pressing play loads a real YouTube embed in the jukebox; pause/skip and the progress line are simulated (no IFrame API in the mockup). Google sign-in and “YouTube Premium” are stubs; likes, never-play and the resume point are stored per concept in localStorage.
  • The feelings box matches words from feelings.json; the in-browser language model of the live site is left out here.
  • The Bengali date is approximate (fixed Gregorian month starts, the same cut-offs the live site uses for seasons).
