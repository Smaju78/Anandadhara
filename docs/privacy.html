Privacy

Anandadhara is a free, personal, non-commercial website about the songs of Rabindranath Tagore. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not collect personal information.

What is stored, and where

Your likes, the songs you chose never to play, your jukebox filters and a short list of recently played songs are saved in your own browser (local storage). They never leave your device, and you can remove them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. Nothing is stored on a server.

Deleting your data

To delete everything, use the browser setting that clears site data for smaju78.github.io. The YouTube data this site keeps (video IDs, titles, channel names and view and like counts) is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days; videos that are removed from YouTube are dropped at the next refresh.

YouTube

Recordings are played with the embedded YouTube player, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). When you play a video, YouTube and Google receive information as described in the Google Privacy Policy, and your use of the player is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. This site uses YouTube API Services to find recordings; that lookup runs offline, not in your browser.

Ask AI

The "Ask AI: meaning" button opens ChatGPT (chatgpt.com, run by OpenAI) in a new tab with a question about the song already written, including its title and Bengali lyrics. Nothing is sent until you click it, and nothing about you is added. What happens next is covered by OpenAI's own terms and privacy policy.

Other services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and video thumbnails from YouTube's image servers (i.ytimg.com). The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

Song data

Song information comes from geetabitan.com and Bengali Wikisource. Stored video details (titles, channels, view and like counts) are refreshed at least every 30 days.
